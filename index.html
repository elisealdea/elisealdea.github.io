Selected Projects

Fluid Mechanics · Mechanisms

Hydraulic Robotic Arm — Syringe-Powered Manipulator

Designed and built a multi-degree-of-freedom robotic arm powered entirely by hydraulic actuators made from syringes and tubing. The arm is mounted on a wheeled Tonka truck chassis with wooden dowel outriggers for stability. A binder-clip gripper end-effector (fabricated in-class) can pick and place objects. The project explored Pascal's law, fluid power transmission, and mechanical linkage geometry across multiple build iterations.

Dynamics · Mechanisms

Jungle Automata — Cam-Driven Kinetic Sculpture

Designed and built a hand-cranked automata with a jungle theme. 3D-printed cams and followers inside a painted wooden box animate a swinging monkey and a flapping butterfly, choreographed through cam profile geometry and follower displacement diagrams.

Manufacturing · Woodworking

Wood Wildlife Sculpture — Giraffe

Hand-fabricated a 3D giraffe sculpture in hardwood from sketch to finished piece. Used the interlocking two-profile scroll-saw technique to achieve full 3D form from flat stock, finished with progressive sanding and gloss oil.

Manufacturing · Lathe

Turned Hardwood Bowl

Turned a hardwood bowl on a wood lathe from rough stock. Project required understanding material removal rates, tool angle and geometry, and surface finish progression. Wall thickness was controlled by feel and calipers to achieve uniform thickness throughout.
